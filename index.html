Jamboo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Jamboo is an independent music player for Jellyfin media servers. It is not made by or affiliated with the Jellyfin project. This policy explains what Jamboo stores and where.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Jamboo doesn’t sell or share your data, and the app’s developer doesn’t receive any of it.
  • Jamboo only talks to the Jellyfin server you choose to sign in to.

What stays on your device

Your server address, a sign-in key from your server (never your password), your settings, listening statistics, recent searches and any music you download. Deleting the app removes all of this.

What’s stored on the server you sign in to

Your Jellyfin server keeps your account, favorites, playlists and play counts, as Jellyfin always does.

If you create an account through Jamboo’s sign-up screen, that server’s sign-up helper also stores your first and last name, email address and (optionally) phone number. They are used only so the server owner can approve your account and so you can reset your password by email. They’re visible only to that server’s administrators.

The person or organization running each server is responsible for the data on it. For servers run by Nick Chu, contact nickchu954@gmail.com.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, email and phone in Settings → Account → Your profile.
  • Delete your account and the personal details stored with it in Settings → Account → Delete my account.
  • Remove downloads and statistics on your device in Settings.

Children

Jamboo isn’t directed at children under 13, and its sign-up isn’t intended for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
